CRISPR-Cas9 editing of TP53 reveals a role in organoid growth

Abstract

Mitochondria are the powerhouse of the cell and produce most of its ATP [1]. Here we show that loss of TP53 in Mus musculus organoids increases growth by 35% (p < 0.05; see Figure 1).
